Drive safely: Solo Together and meet-ups

Solo Together cruise nights are an online community activity. Each member drives on their own, wherever they live, and shares their drive afterward. DaClassicLady doesn't organize, supervise or control anyone's drive, route or vehicle.

  • Obey all traffic laws and posted speed limits. The club is about unwinding, not racing.
  • Never film, post, text or use your phone while driving. Use a mounted camera, and share your drive after you've parked.
  • Make sure your vehicle is safe, insured and legally registered.
  • Don't share your home address or real-time location publicly. Post your drive after you've left the spot.
  • Meet-ups hosted by members are independent. DaClassicLady doesn't host or supervise them unless we clearly say so. Use good judgment when meeting people from online.
